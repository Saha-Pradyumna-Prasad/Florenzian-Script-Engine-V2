Inspiration

    
The spirit of Florence and the legacy of the Brotherhood.

    
    

      

        
The Assassin's Creed Legacy

        
This project is heavily inspired by the rich history of the Italian Renaissance, brought to life in the legendary game Assassin's Creed: Brotherhood. Just as Ezio Auditore navigated the intricate streets of Florence and the majestic ruins of Rome, we wanted to build a system that honors the art, architecture and intellect of this era.

        
From the grand domes of the Florence Cathedral to the ancient Roman Colosseum, our engine's aesthetic and functionality are a tribute to the city that sparked the Renaissance.
